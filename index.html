NVIDIA AI Transparency & Integration Details

MediAssist AI uses a secure Node.js backend integrated with NVIDIA NIM AI for general health education and patient visit preparation. Health education questions entered in the chat are transmitted to NVIDIA's AI service to generate responses. Personal planner data (symptoms, appointments, and medication schedules) is saved exclusively in your browser's local storage.

How the Server-Side NVIDIA AI Integration Operates:

1

Secure Backend Proxy (Node.js & Express)

Your browser communicates only with your local server. Your private NVIDIA API key is stored safely in server environment variables (.env) and is never exposed to browser JavaScript.

2

Server-Side AI Model Inference

When you ask a question, the backend securely forwards your prompt to NVIDIA NIM's API to generate plain-language health explanations and doctor discussion questions.

3

Strict Non-Diagnostic Clinical Safety Guardrails

System instructions constrain the AI: it adopts a supportive educational tone, explicitly refuses to provide definitive medical diagnoses or prescribe medications, and directs emergency red flags immediately to 911 / 112.

4

Zero Unnecessary Medical Data Storage

The backend does not store, log, or persist user medical questions to any database or disk files, ensuring minimal data retention.
